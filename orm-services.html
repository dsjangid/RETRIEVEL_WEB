Targeted Post-Level Content Removals

Often, a reputational attack does not involve an entire profile, but rather a single viral reel, a defamatory story, or a malicious image uploaded by an influential creator. When you report the post through the app, platforms rarely take action because high-engagement posts generate advertising revenue.

We operate through media partner rights manager tools and internal trust and safety channels. We isolate the specific URL, document copyright, privacy, and harassment infractions, and secure direct server-level deletion of the individual post within hours without affecting the creator's account.

Reddit Discussion Thread Eradication

Due to Google’s search algorithms, Reddit threads often rank in top search positions. Anonymous Reddit users can post unverified claims, doxx personal information, or publish defamatory commentary that remains visible indefinitely.

We submit legal and privacy takedown claims directly to Reddit’s legal and trust & safety teams under doxxing, harassment, and trademark infringement policies to permanently wipe offending threads and comments.

5-Star Google Reviews & Rating Restoration

A wave of fake 1-star reviews from competitors or disgruntled individuals can drop your Google Maps rating and destroy customer trust. We challenge and appeal fraudulent reviews directly through Google Business support channels to have them removed.

Additionally, we deploy authentic, GEO-targeted, verified 5-star customer reviews from active Google accounts to strengthen your overall rating and ensure your business consistently displays a clean 4.9+ star score.
