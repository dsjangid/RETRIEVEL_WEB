Why Facebook Ad Accounts and Business Managers Get Disabled

Facebook’s systems are built around automated risk detection. Accounts may be disabled due to policy misunderstandings, flagged ad creatives, payment disputes, suspicious activity, or linked asset violations. In many cases, advertisers receive limited explanation.

A Business Manager restriction can be even more serious. When Business Manager is disabled, it can affect:

  • Ad accounts

  • Facebook Pages

  • Instagram accounts

  • Payment methods

  • Pixels and tracking data

  • Team member access

The impact spreads across the entire advertising ecosystem.
