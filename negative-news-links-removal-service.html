Google De-Indexing & Website Takedowns Desk

We permanently remove negative news articles, outdated legal links, and defamatory content from Google Search results, and execute host-level takedowns for rogue websites.

Direct to Telegram

How Negative Links Damage Personal & Corporate Credibility

When prospective clients, business partners, banks, or employers search your name or company on Google, the first page of search results determines their decision. Even a single misleading news article, outdated legal allegation, or defamatory blog post can cause millions in lost revenue or derail your career.

Webmasters and news editors rarely agree to take down published articles voluntarily, and many demand extortionate removal fees. Attempting to suppress negative links with ordinary SEO can take years and is rarely permanent.

Our Direct Google Search De-Indexing Protocol

Rather than negotiating with hostile webmasters, RETRIEVAL submits formal legal de-indexing filings directly to Google’s Legal Removals and Search Quality departments under international Right to Erasure (GDPR), defamation, copyright, and privacy frameworks.

Once Google Legal approves the filing, the offending URL is permanently de-listed and removed from Google Search worldwide. When anyone searches your name, brand, or executive team, the negative article will never appear. Turnaround time is typically 3 to 7 business days.

Rogue Website & Host Domain Takedowns

When bad actors build entire standalone websites or blog networks specifically to extort, defame, or counterfeit your brand, de-indexing alone is not enough—the website itself must be taken down.

We initiate host-level DMCA and domain registrar compliance enforcement across infrastructure providers such as Cloudflare, AWS, GoDaddy, and Namecheap. We submit verified evidence of fraud, copyright infringement, and abuse, securing complete server shutdowns and domain suspensions within 2 to 5 days.

The De-Indexing Process

1. URL Review: Send us the search queries and the exact URLs appearing in Google search results.

2. Case Construction: We prepare the formal Right of Erasure & legal violation dossier.

3. Google Submission: The dossier is submitted directly to Google’s legal team for expedited review.

4. Complete De-Listing: The URL disappears from search results, and funds in escrow are released only after you verify the search index is clean.
